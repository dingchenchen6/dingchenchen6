## 👋 About Me

Chenchen Ding (丁晨晨) is a biodiversity researcher working on **conservation biology**, **global change biology**, and **biogeography**. Her research focuses on understanding and predicting the impacts of global changes (e.g., land-use and climate change) on biodiversity, species distributions, community structure, ecosystem services, and conservation priority identification.

**Current Position:** Peking University - IIASA International Joint Postdoctoral Fellow  
**PhD:** Ecology, Beijing Normal University (2024)  
**Research Keywords:** `species distribution modeling` · `occupancy analysis` · `biodiversity monitoring` · `ecological modeling` · `extinction risk` · `PREDICTS database` · `functional diversity`
